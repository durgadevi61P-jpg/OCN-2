# Exp.No:2 Voltage-Current Characteristics of LED/LASER

## Fiber Optic LED and LASER Characteristics

### AIM

- To study and plot the V-I characteristics of fiber optic LED (850 nm) source for CW.
- To study and plot the V-I characteristics of fiber optic LASER (1310 nm) source for CW.

---

### EQUIPMENTS REQUIRED

- FOM-1A module, Optical patch cords.
- FOM-1B module, Optical patch cords.

---

### PROCEDURE FOR LED CHARACTERISTICS

1. Make sure that the FOM-1A module is OFF.
2. Keep the intensity control pot fully anticlockwise.
3. Keep the signal selector switch in CW position.
4. Turn ON the FOM-1A module. The current display should show 00.0 and the voltage display should show the garbage value.
5. Turn the intensity control pot slightly in the clockwise direction until the current display shows 00.1 mA.
6. Record the current in mA and the corresponding voltage in volts.
7. Repeat the procedure for current readings in steps of 00.1 mA up to 45 mA. Record the current in mA and the corresponding voltage in volts.
8. Plot the graph for current (mA) vs. voltage (V).
9. Find out the Threshold Current (I_th) in mA from the I-V graph.

### PROCEDURE FOR LASER CHARACTERISTICS

1. Make sure that the FOM-1B module is OFF.
2. Keep the intensity control pot fully anticlockwise.
3. Keep the signal selector switch in CW position.
4. Turn ON the FOM-1B module. The current display should show 00.0 and the voltage display should show the garbage value.
5. Turn the intensity control pot slightly in the clockwise direction until the current display shows 00.1 mA.
6. Record the current in mA and the corresponding voltage in volts.
7. Repeat the procedure for current readings in steps of 00.1 mA up to 45 mA. Record the current in mA and the corresponding voltage in volts.
8. Plot the graph for current (mA) vs. voltage (V).
9. Find out the Threshold Current (I_th) in mA from the I-V graph.

### THEORY

In an optical fiber communication system, the electrical signal is first converted into an optical signal using an **E/O conversion device** such as an LED. The optical signal is transmitted through the fiber and then retrieved in its original electrical form using an **O/E conversion device** such as a photodetector.

**Key Points:**

- **LEDs:**
  - Emitters vary due to chip fabrication technologies.
  - Important parameters: peak wavelength, conversion efficiency, optical rise/fall times, maximum forward current, and forward voltage.
  - Linear optical output with respect to forward current in a certain region.

- **Photodetectors:**
  - Types: photoconductive, photovoltaic, transistor-type, and diode-type.
  - Important parameters: response time, wavelength sensitivity, and responsivity.

- **Numerical Aperture (NA):**
  - Defines the acceptance cone of the fiber.
  - Light must enter within this cone to be transmitted properly; otherwise, it refracts out of the core.
